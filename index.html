Engineering Profile

Masanso David studies Software Engineering at Cavendish University Uganda while running active field operations through Masa Enterprise — an ISP and technical services outfit — and MasaDroid, his Android modding and repair hub. That combination means his engineering is tested twice: once in code review, once on-site with real routers, real handsets, and real bandwidth constraints in Namanve.

He moves comfortably between application layers and physical infrastructure — writing the backend that runs a service, then pulling cable or flashing firmware to make sure it actually reaches the people using it. He currently works as a Network Engineer at Masa Enterprise in Mukono & Namanve, engineering network infrastructure and maintaining business hardware and software systems.
